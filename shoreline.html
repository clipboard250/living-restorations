Why We Gather

Raise a glass for our coastlines

This is an intimate evening at The Study, St. Petersburg's pour-your-own wine and bourbon lounge, where community, conversation, and conservation come together. Every ticket supports local living shoreline projects that protect and restore the coastal ecosystems the Tampa Bay region depends on.

Living shorelines are natural coastal restoration efforts that strengthen habitat, improve water quality, and build resilience against storms and erosion. Keeping them healthy takes ongoing maintenance and monitoring, and that is exactly what your support helps make possible.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need before the evening at The Study.

When and where is the fundraiser?
Thursday, July 30, 2026, from 6:00 to 9:00 PM at The Study Wine & Bourbon Tasting Lounge, 3100 3rd Avenue North, St. Petersburg, FL 33713.
How much are tickets?
Tickets are $20 per person, and $10 of each ticket is tax-deductible. You can purchase online through Donorbox.
What is the difference between the raffle and the silent auction?
Almost everything is raffled: a private magic show, handcrafted charcuterie boards, native plants, a design consultation, a Golfology gift bag, and a bottle of Gold Bar Bourbon. Each prize has its own bucket, so you drop your tickets toward what you want to win. Tickets are 1 for $10, 3 for $25, 8 for $50, or 20 for $100, and you must be present to win. The one silent auction item is West Evans's original painting of Cockroach Bay, created live during the event, with bidding starting at a $400 minimum.
Is food and drink included?
Guests receive a complimentary glass of champagne on arrival and a charcuterie spread. The Study's wine and bourbon selections are available on a pay-as-you-go basis.
What does the event support?
Proceeds support living shoreline restoration in the Tampa Bay region, benefiting Ecosphere Restoration Institute's ongoing maintenance and monitoring work.
Can I help if I cannot attend?
Yes. An online donation drive runs alongside the event for supporters who cannot make it in person.
