Fine Spirits Meet Coastal Conservation at July 30 St. Petersburg Fundraiser

Ecosphere Restoration Institute and Living Spaces Gardening host an evening benefit at The Study to support living shoreline restoration in the Tampa Bay region.

ST. PETERSBURG, Fla. — [RELEASE DATE] Ecosphere Restoration Institute, in partnership with Living Spaces Gardening, will host the Living Shoreline Summer Fundraiser on Thursday, July 30, 2026, from 6:00 to 9:00 PM at The Study Wine & Bourbon Tasting Lounge in St. Petersburg. The evening pairs an elegant social gathering with a clear purpose: raising funds to maintain and monitor the living shoreline projects that protect the region's coastlines.

Guests will be welcomed with a complimentary glass of champagne and close-up magic from St. Petersburg magician John Williams, followed by a talk from Tom Ries on what living shorelines are, why they matter, and why ongoing care is essential to their long-term success. The night continues with a raffle of prizes donated by local businesses and artists, live music, a charcuterie spread, and The Study's pour-your-own wine and bourbon selections available on a pay-as-you-go basis.

"Living shorelines protect our waterways, strengthen habitat, and build resilience, but keeping them healthy takes ongoing maintenance," said [SPEAKER NAME, TITLE]. "This evening is a chance for the community to come together, enjoy a memorable night, and directly support that work."

About Ecosphere Restoration Institute

Ecosphere Restoration Institute is a nonprofit environmental organization with deep roots in the Tampa Bay region, dedicated to restoring coastal and freshwater ecosystems through science-based, nature-driven solutions.

About Living Spaces Gardening

Living Spaces Gardening is a St. Petersburg-based native landscaping and habitat restoration company founded in 2018. Led by Florida Master Naturalist Kiamesha Wray, the company designs and installs Florida native plant landscapes that support local ecosystems for residential and commercial clients.
