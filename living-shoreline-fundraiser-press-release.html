St. Petersburg Benefit Raises Funds to Keep Tampa Bay's Living Shorelines Alive

A July 30 evening at The Study pairs a raffle, a silent auction, and close-up magic with a cause most people never hear about: the upkeep that keeps coastal restoration working.

ST. PETERSBURG, Fla. ([RELEASE DATE]) Living shorelines protect Tampa Bay's coast by replacing hardened seawalls with native plants, oysters, and natural materials that absorb storm energy and rebuild habitat. Building them is only half the work. Keeping them healthy takes years of maintenance and monitoring that rarely comes with funding, and that gap is what a new St. Petersburg benefit sets out to close.

The Living Shoreline Summer Fundraiser will be held Thursday, July 30, 2026, from 6:00 to 9:00 PM at The Study Wine and Bourbon Tasting Lounge. Hosted by Living Restorations in partnership with Living Spaces Gardening, the evening benefits Ecosphere Restoration Institute, the Tampa Bay nonprofit that carries out and maintains local shoreline projects.

Guests are welcomed with a complimentary glass of champagne and close-up magic from St. Petersburg magician John Williams, who also hosts the evening as master of ceremonies. Tom Ries then presents the Cockroach Bay living shoreline restoration, and artist West Evans paints that same shoreline live throughout the night. The finished original headlines a silent auction, while nearly everything else, including the magic show and handcrafted charcuterie boards, is raffled, alongside a charcuterie spread and The Study's pour-your-own wine and bourbon on a pay-as-you-go basis.

[DRAFT QUOTE for Kiamesha Wray to approve or revise:] "People assume a restoration project is finished the day it goes in the ground, but the first few years of care are what make it last," said Kiamesha Wray, owner of Living Restorations and a Florida Master Naturalist. "This evening lets the community be part of that work, and have a genuinely good night out while they do it."

About Ecosphere Restoration Institute

Ecosphere Restoration Institute is a nonprofit environmental organization with deep roots in the Tampa Bay region, dedicated to restoring coastal and freshwater ecosystems through science-based, nature-driven solutions.

About Living Restorations

Living Restorations provides ecological design and coastal restoration services in the Tampa Bay and St. Petersburg, Florida area. The company is led by Kiamesha Wray, a Florida Master Naturalist since 2014 and one of the first FANN credentialed professionals in Florida, and holds an ISNetworld B-grade contractor rating for commercial work.

About Living Spaces Gardening

Founded in 2018 by Kiamesha Wray, Living Spaces Gardening designs and installs Florida native plant landscapes for residential and commercial clients across the Tampa Bay region. The company was featured in Homes and Gardens magazine in November 2025.
