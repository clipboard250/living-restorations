Fine Spirits Meet Coastal Conservation at July 30 St. Petersburg Fundraiser

Living Restorations and Living Spaces Gardening host an evening benefit at The Study, with a raffle and silent auction supporting living shoreline restoration in the Tampa Bay region.

ST. PETERSBURG, Fla. ([RELEASE DATE]) Living Restorations, in partnership with Living Spaces Gardening, will host the Living Shoreline Summer Fundraiser on Thursday, July 30, 2026, from 6:00 to 9:00 PM at The Study Wine and Bourbon Tasting Lounge in St. Petersburg. The evening pairs an elegant social gathering with a clear purpose: raising funds to maintain and monitor the living shoreline projects that protect the region's coastlines, in support of Ecosphere Restoration Institute.

Guests will be welcomed with a complimentary glass of champagne and close-up magic from St. Petersburg magician John Williams, followed by a talk from Tom Ries on what living shorelines are, why they matter, and why ongoing care is essential to their long-term success. The night continues with a raffle and a silent auction of prizes donated by local businesses and artists, a charcuterie spread, and The Study's pour-your-own wine and bourbon selections available on a pay-as-you-go basis.

[DRAFT QUOTE for Kiamesha Wray to approve or revise:] "Living shorelines protect our waterways, strengthen habitat, and build resilience, but keeping them healthy takes ongoing care," said Kiamesha Wray, owner of Living Restorations and a Florida Master Naturalist. "This evening is a chance for the community to come together, enjoy a memorable night, and directly support that work."

Event Partners & Donors

About Living Restorations

Living Restorations provides ecological design and coastal restoration services in the Tampa Bay and St. Petersburg, Florida area. The company is led by Kiamesha Wray, a Florida Master Naturalist since 2014 and one of the first FANN credentialed professionals in Florida, focusing on science-based environmental restoration.

About Living Spaces Gardening

Founded in 2018 by Kiamesha Wray, Living Spaces Gardening designs and installs Florida native plant landscapes for residential and commercial clients across the Tampa Bay region. The company was featured in Homes and Gardens magazine in November 2025.

About Ecosphere Restoration Institute

Ecosphere Restoration Institute is a nonprofit environmental organization with deep roots in the Tampa Bay region, dedicated to restoring coastal and freshwater ecosystems through science-based, nature-driven solutions.
