What We Do

Every service, built around your ecosystem.

We don't install generic designs. Every project starts with a deep read of your site — the soil, the hydrology, the species that belong there.

Invasive Removal

Strategic removal of invasive species to give native ecosystems room to recover — and stay recovered.

Dune Systems

Design and restoration of coastal dune habitat — Florida's first line of defense against erosion and storm surge.

Coastal Restoration

Comprehensive ecological restoration from tidal zones to upland transition areas — the full picture.

Native Plant Sourcing

Florida-native plant sourcing for restoration and design — the right species for the right place, every time.

Environmental Design

Thoughtful site design that integrates ecological function with human use and lasting habitat value.

Living Shoreline Design & Install

Natural shoreline stabilization using oyster reefs, native grasses, and organic materials — protecting property while restoring habitat.

Maintenance & Monitoring

Long-term stewardship keeps your restoration on track. We monitor, adapt, and maintain so your investment grows stronger over time — not just for one season, but for years.

Our Approach

Science-backed.
Site-specific.
Built to last.

We don't install generic designs. Every Living Restorations project starts with a deep read of your site — the soil, the hydrology, the existing species, the pressures it faces.

Then we design and build something that establishes itself, adapts, and becomes more resilient over time. That's what ecological design actually means.
